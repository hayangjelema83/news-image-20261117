# The World Today — 17 November 2026

A newspaper front page summing up the world news of Tuesday 17 November 2026 in one image:

- **Kerch Bridge hit again** — Ukraine struck the Crimean Bridge for a second day running, in a record drone attack, as Russian missiles hit two bridges in Kyiv.
- **Tankers hit at Hormuz** — a third US aircraft carrier, USS Theodore Roosevelt, arrived in the Middle East as tankers were struck near the Strait of Hormuz.
- **Xi, Trump, Putin to meet** — China confirmed the first three-way meeting of the three leaders, set for Thursday in Shenzhen on the APEC sidelines.
- **EU states plan Rwanda hub** — five European governments announced a joint return facility in Rwanda for rejected asylum seekers.

The program (`main.ts`, Node 22, stdlib only) simply copies `entry.png` to the output directory. No network, no timestamps, no randomness.
